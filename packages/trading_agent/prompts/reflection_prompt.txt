## REFLECTION INSTRUCTIONS

You must follow these rules strictly:

**Rule 1: Ground in facts only**
You are given the exact original reasoning and the actual PnL. Do not rationalize errors. Do not invent signals that weren't in the original reasoning. If the trade lost money despite a BUY decision, acknowledge it directly.

**Rule 2: Signal-level accountability (required)**
For every signal mentioned in the original reasoning (RSI, MACD, sentiment, volume, ADX, etc.), determine whether that signal CORRECTLY predicted the subsequent price direction. Populate signal_accuracy accordingly:
- True = the signal correctly predicted the direction
- False = the signal was wrong or misleading

**Rule 3: Memory storage filter**
Set should_store_memory = True ONLY if the absolute PnL is greater than 2.0% (either direction). Trades within ±2% are inconclusive and storing them dilutes future memory retrieval quality.

**Rule 4: Action-oriented lessons (required)**
Write lessons_learned as a specific, actionable trading rule in this format:
"In [regime] regime, when [specific indicator/condition], prefer [action] because [reason]."
Avoid vague statements like "be more careful" or "analysis was incomplete."

**Rule 5: Importance scoring**
Assign higher memory_importance_score (closer to 1.0) for:
- Trades where multiple conflicting signals were present
- High-volatility regime trades
- Situations where the outcome strongly contradicted the prediction
- Edge cases not well-represented in existing memories

Assign lower scores (closer to 0.0) for:
- Trades with clear, well-aligned signals
- Outcomes that matched the prediction well
- Common market conditions already well-represented

**Rule 6: prediction_vs_reality (required)**
Find the falsifiable prediction in the original reasoning (the "PREDICTION: I expect..." statement) and directly compare it to what actually happened. Quote the prediction and state the actual outcome.

Your output must conform strictly to the ReflectionOutput schema. Be objective, specific, and constructive.
